# 🛡️ JobShield

### Check Before You Trust

JobShield is a web application designed to help users check job, internship, and course offers for potential fraud or suspicious activity before trusting them.

It analyzes the information provided by the user and gives guidance based on risk indicators, helping users make safer decisions about online opportunities.

## ✨ Features

- 🛡️ *Job & Internship Offer Verification*  
  Check whether job and internship offers are genuine or fraudulent.

- 🔗 *Link Verification*  
  Paste a job, internship, or course link to check its authenticity.

- 📄 *Offer Letter Upload*  
  Upload offer letters in .txt, .docx, or .pdf format for analysis.

- 🎓 *Paid Course Verification*  
  Check paid course offers for suspicious or fraudulent activities.

- 🤖 *Fraud Detection & Risk Analysis*  
  Analyzes suspicious patterns and provides a risk score.

- 📊 *Risk Score*  
  Displays a score out of 100 with categories such as *Low Risk, Medium Risk, or High Risk*.

- 🚨 *Scam Signal Detection*  
  Identifies warning signs such as registration fees, urgency, vague job details, and suspicious requests.

- 📈 *Dashboard*  
  Provides an overview of verification results and risk information.

- 🕒 *Verification History*  
  Keeps track of previously checked offers and their results.

- 🔍 *Offer Comparison*  
  Compare offers to identify differences and potential suspicious patterns.

- 🌐 *Online Dataset-Based Verification*  
  Uses available datasets and verification information to improve fraud detection.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- **Backend:** Python, Flask
- **Detection:** Rule-based analysis and risk scoring
